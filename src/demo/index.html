What it may and may not do

  • It cannot quote below the floor. Not under pressure, not after repeated asking — the number is refused before it can be spoken.
  • It cannot undercut you. Say what you pay now and it will never offer less than that.
  • It cannot promise capacity it does not have.
  • There is nobody to escalate to. No "let me ask my manager" — the limits are absolute.
  • It cannot mark its own work done. Every claim is born UNVERIFIED and only independent evidence promotes it.

Stack

Telephony — a1mobile number over their SIP trunk (Telnyx), outbound via LiveKit SIP.
Voice — Deepgram nova-3 → OpenAI gpt-5.4 → Cartesia sonic-2, Silero VAD on-device.
Control — a state graph gates every tool: quantity before capacity, capacity before price, validated price before close. Illegal orderings are unreachable rather than discouraged.
Money — a cost model owns the floor; the agent proposes and a validator disposes.
Proof — Claim → Evidence → Receipt. Only channels the agent cannot write to can verify. A receipt hand-edited to say VERIFIED re-derives as UNVERIFIED.
